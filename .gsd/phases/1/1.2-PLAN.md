  <files>

  <action>
    Evaluate Arduino vs. ESP-IDF strictly against the prioritized criteria defined in `.gsd/DECISIONS.md`:
    1. Bluetooth HID device capability and implementation quality (BLE mouse reports, buttons, wheel/scroll, future keyboard/media composite reports, pairing/reconnect stability, API maturity).
    2. Resource usage and available headroom (integrating Plan 1.1 empirical flash and RAM metrics).
    3. Long-term maintainability and architectural fit.
    4. Development velocity and peripheral ecosystem (MPU6050, NVS).
    5. Build/configuration complexity and reproducibility.
    6. Host-testability and framework-independent HAL boundary (`IImuDriver`, `IHidMouse`, `IStorageService`, `IInputDevice`).
    
    Document the detailed evaluation points and comparative trade-offs in `.gsd/phases/1/EVALUATION_NOTES.md`.
    
    AVOID: Relying on popularity or subjective bias.
    USE: Grounded architectural comparison backed by Plan 1.1 empirical data.
  </action>

  </verify>
